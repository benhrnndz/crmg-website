Legislative Mission & Governance Vision

Priority Educational Initiative
AY 2026–2027

CHED Tulong Dunong Program (TDP) Scholarship

The CHED Tulong Dunong Program (TDP) is a national scholarship grant administered in close coordination with the Commission on Higher Education. Endorsed through the Office of Congressman Roy M. Gonzales, this program delivers direct financial stipends for tuition, academic fees, and living allowances to qualified college students from Santa Rosa.

Core Program Benefits

  • Direct semesterly allowance for books and school supplies
  • Tuition subsidy for CHED-accredited private & local colleges
  • Protection against financial dropouts for underprivileged families
  • Priority endorsement for Santa Rosa resident scholars

Eligibility Criteria

  • Certified bona fide resident of the Lone District of Santa Rosa
  • Currently enrolled in a CHED-recognized higher education institution
  • General Weighted Average (GWA) of 2.50 or better
  • Combined annual family income within official CHED poverty thresholds

Comprehensive District Assistance Services

The Congressional Office provides multiple direct social welfare and civic support mechanisms.

Medical & Hospitalization Assistance

Issuance of Guarantee Letters (GL) covering medicines, diagnostic laboratory tests, hemodialysis, and emergency hospitalization for indigent Santa Rosa constituents in accredited partner hospitals.

Required: Clinical Abstract, Hospital Billing Statement, Barangay Certificate

Livelihood & Skills Upgrading

Vocational scholarships in partnership with TESDA, alongside short-term emergency community employment (TUPAD) to empower displaced workers, homemakers, and transport cooperatives.

Required: Valid Government ID, Certificate of Non-Employment or Indigency

District Infrastructure Development

Direct allocation of national infrastructure appropriations for barangay roads, multi-purpose covered courts, river wall embankments, and stormwater pumping facilities throughout Santa Rosa.

Channel: Barangay Council Endorsement Resolution to Congressional Office

Senior Citizens Welfare & Health Subsidies

Dedicated social protection for elderly residents, featuring free chronic disease maintenance medication distribution, regular geriatric wellness checkups, and legal advisory services.

Required: OSCA Senior Citizen ID, Medical Prescription
